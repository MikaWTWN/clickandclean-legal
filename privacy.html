Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Click & Clean ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Profile information (photo, bio, experience)
  • Location data (to show nearby cleaners and services)
  • Payment information (processed securely through Stripe)
  • Communication data (messages within the app)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • Usage data (features used, time spent in app)
  • Log data (IP address, access times)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Connect customers with cleaning service providers
  • Process payments securely
  • Send notifications about bookings and messages
  • Improve our app and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Between users: Your profile information is visible to other users to facilitate service bookings
  • Service providers: We use third-party services including:
    • Stripe for payment processing
    • Firebase for authentication and data storage
    • Socket.io for real-time messaging
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure payment processing through Stripe
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication

6. Your Rights (GDPR/AVG Compliance)

Under EU and Dutch law, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Cookie Policy

Our app uses minimal cookies and local storage for:

  • Authentication and session management
  • User preferences (dark mode, language)

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download the Click & Clean app, tap "Sign Up", and follow the prompts to create either a Customer or ZZP (service provider) account.

How does payment work?

We use a secure escrow system. When you book a service, payment is held securely and only released to the service provider after the job is completed to your satisfaction.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards through our secure payment partner, Stripe. Your payment information is never stored on our servers.

What are the fees?

For customers: The platform is free to use. You only pay for the cleaning services you book.
For ZZP providers: We charge a 10% commission on completed jobs.

How do I cancel a booking?

Go to your Planning/Calendar tab, find the booking, and tap "Cancel". Cancellations more than 24 hours in advance receive a full refund. Late cancellations may incur fees.

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

Contact us within 48 hours of service completion at support@clickandclean.app. We'll review your case and work towards a fair resolution.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. We take data security seriously and comply with GDPR/AVG regulations. Read our Privacy Policy above for details.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is permanent and will remove all your data.

The app is not working properly. What should I do?

Try these steps:

  1. Make sure you have the latest version of the app
  2. Restart the app
  3. Check your internet connection
  4. Restart your device

If problems persist, contact us at support@clickandclean.app
